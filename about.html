About KROTAL

Autonomous FX trading systems, engineered for prop-firm survival.

What is KROTAL?

KROTAL is an autonomous FX trading system. It deploys self-operating trading agents onto dedicated VPS infrastructure — purpose-built to pass FTMO prop-firm challenges and run live capital. The stack is driven by an LLM decision engine (CentCom) that ingests market data, produces ranked trade ideas with sized risk, and routes orders to your broker around the clock.

Every deployment enforces FTMO challenge rules at the protocol level: max daily loss, max overall loss, weekend and news lockouts, and trade-frequency throttles — so the system works within the constraints of your challenge, not against them.

The product lineup

KROTAL Centurion

The full autonomous trading system on a dedicated Contabo Windows VPS, FTMO 200K · 3-Step optimized, with managed updates and monitoring.

PKG_B

KROTAL Kopier

A trade copier that mirrors CentCom master signals to your own FTMO account — proportional lot sizing, SL/TP and timing. No LLM keys required.

PKG_C

KROTAL Krowd

Group crowdsourced trading — pool resources with other traders, share infrastructure, keep your own FTMO account. Up to 12 seats per pool.

The pipeline

  • 01 · INGEST — market data streamed into the KROTAL LLM decision engine.
  • 02 · DECIDE — CentCom produces ranked trade ideas with sized risk.
  • 03 · EXECUTE — orders routed through dedicated VPS to your FTMO broker.
  • 04 · GUARD — DD-guard, news lock and kill-switch enforce challenge rules.
